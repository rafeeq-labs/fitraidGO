# World-map concept prompts

## Master GPS street-map prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: full-resolution vertical 9:16 GPS-based fantasy MMO map view, no UI.

Primary request: One complete, polished GPS world view of a [BIOME] city or settlement. It must be based on believable real-world street geometry: a long main avenue runs upward from the player, branching at a 4-way intersection and a T-junction into rectangular housing blocks; include a public park or square, a water feature appropriate to the biome, and dense but readable residential parcels. Preserve this street-map structure while rendering it as a high-end fantasy MMO. Use biome-specific road materials, terrain, vegetation, water, and modular buildings. Include a visible mix of Level 1 starter cottages, Level 2 homes, and a few Level 3 manors or guild buildings.

Place one small cloaked player avatar at the bottom centre, facing up the main road, inside a large translucent blue GPS interaction-radius circle. Streets and blocks should extend ahead toward the distance.

Style/medium: premium stylized PBR isometric mobile MMO world; clear, readable game environment; crisp materials and strong silhouette separation.
Composition/framing: vertical 9:16, high oblique GPS street-map camera, player low on screen, no UI.

Constraints: use only 18–25 larger, clearly separated buildings. Use wide clean streets, large readable blocks, controlled detail, clean negative space, crisp edges, no visual clutter.
Avoid: smudgy texture, blurry buildings, excessive micro-detail, dense repeated houses, painterly blur, text, labels, logos, watermark, or assets from existing games.
```

## Snow-city values

Use `[BIOME] = snowbound northern city` and add: `blue-grey snow-dusted cobblestone roads; timber-and-stone northern homes with steep snow roofs; frost-covered conifers; warm lanterns; turquoise icy canal with two stone bridges; rectangular snow-covered park with a blue crystal landmark; distant cold mountains.`

## Biome substitutions

- Temperate canal city: mossy stone roads, willow-lined canal, green public gardens, slate roofs.
- Alpine mining town: switchback roads, granite walls, pine forest, ore carts, mountain stream.
- Autumn lakeside town: amber trees, dark lake, wooden boardwalks, warm stone roofs.
- Farmland village: dirt-and-cobble lanes, fields, hedges, windmill, irrigation ditch.
- Desert oasis: sandstone roads, palms, canals, walled courtyards, turquoise pools.
- Tropical coast: bright stone streets, palms, harbor docks, beaches, clear blue water.
- Swamp settlement: raised paths, water channels, cypress trees, reed beds, stilt foundations.
