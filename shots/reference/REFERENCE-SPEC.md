# RaidFit Visual Reference Specification

This file is the **authoritative written transcription of the five supplied AAA MMO benchmark
images**. Reviewers/critics who cannot see the originals MUST score captures against this
document. It describes what the target looks like in enough detail to judge a screenshot.

---

## Reference A — Portrait GPS hero view (the primary target composition)

Vertical 9:16 frame. A very high 3/4 bird's-eye view over a fantasy town built on a grid of
large land parcels, receding to distant mountains, lakes and horizon sky with soft cumulus.

Composition, bottom to top:
- **Bottom ~30%**: dark evergreen forest and mossy boulders framing the left and right edges;
  a warm-gray **cobblestone path** runs up the centre from the bottom edge, gently curving.
- **Lower third, centred**: the **player** — a small figure (about 3–4% of frame height, roughly
  50–60 px tall in a 900×1600 frame), seen from behind/above, dark navy cloak, walking up the path.
  Clearly readable as a person, but small: the world dominates.
- Around the player: a **single very large, very thin, bright cyan-blue circle** on the ground —
  the GPS interaction radius. Its diameter spans ~95% of the frame width. Line weight is thin
  (2–4 px), luminous, no thick glow bloom, no fill (or barely-there fill). It reads as a clean
  drawn ring lying flat on the terrain, clipped/occluded correctly by trees and rocks.
- **Middle 40%**: a lattice of **big square/rectangular land parcels**, each bounded by a low
  pale-gray **stone kerb wall** (~0.5 m) sometimes topped with dark wooden fence rails. Inside
  each parcel: mown green grass with tiny white/violet wildflowers, and **either one building or
  nothing** (empty parcels are common and deliberate — the layout breathes). Parcels are separated
  by cobblestone streets meeting at clean junctions.
- Buildings: modest cottages with **steep deep-blue slate roofs**, cream plaster + dark timber
  framing, gray stone bases, and **warm gold glowing windows**. One larger **manor/church** in the
  upper-middle with multiple blue conical spires and gold finials.
- Along every street: **lamp posts** — dark stone/iron poles topped with a **glowing blue crystal**,
  several also carrying a **navy banner with a small gold emblem**.
- **Upper third**: the town thins into meadow, then a lake with a shoreline, a bridge, coniferous
  woods, then blue-gray mountain ranges fading into atmospheric haze.
- **Horizon**: three **tall slender blue crystal spire towers**, each emitting a thin vertical
  beam of blue light into the sky — restrained, distant MMO landmarks.
- Lighting: high sun, warm daylight; grass mid-green and slightly desaturated with distance;
  shadows soft, cool blue-violet, never black. Distance haze is pale blue-gray.

## Reference B — Landscape mid-distance view (same world, closer)

Confirms the material language up close:
- Cobblestone roads: warm gray irregular rounded stones, visible per-stone shading, bounded by a
  crisp lighter **stone kerb** strip; grass creeps into the joints at the edges.
- Buildings: two-storey cottages, **blue slate roof tiles laid in overlapping rows**, cream/ochre
  plaster walls, dark timber posts and beams, stone lintels and steps, wooden doors, and
  **warm-gold lit windows** with small mullions. Chimneys are stone with a thin smoke wisp.
- Fences: dark wooden 2-rail fences on stone kerbs; garden beds with violet/white flowers.
- A tall pale **stone city wall** on the right, hung with **navy banners bearing a gold device**,
  topped with a crenellated walk.
- Blue crystal lamp posts and free-standing blue crystal finials on stone plinths line the paths.
- Player: small, dark navy cloak, standing on the road, with a **small tight blue ring** at the feet
  (the near-field version of the GPS ring) — clearly visible against the cobblestone.
- Trees: dark blue-green conifers, dense at the edges; scattered gray boulders; grass with
  yellow/white flowers.
- Lighting: sun from the upper left, warm; long soft shadows across the road; overall exposure is
  rich and slightly filmic, never washed out or muddy.

## Reference C — Modular asset sheet (the modularity/upgrade target)

Isometric assets on a flat dark gray backdrop, all sharing one plot footprint:
- **Row 1 — the upgrade ladder, all on the SAME square plot base**:
  1. **Level 0 plot**: bare grass square, bounded by a pale stone kerb with square corner posts,
     one small wooden fence section. Nothing else. Reads as "buildable land".
  2. **Level 1**: one-storey cottage, blue slate gable roof, single stone chimney with smoke,
     timber-framed cream walls, one door, two windows, a couple of yard props, remaining grass.
  3. **Level 2**: clearly **taller and wider** — two storeys, more elaborate roof with a cross
     gable, more windows, visible timber framing, stone base course, planted garden, wood pile,
     a bench. Same kerb, same plot.
  4. **Level 3**: **prestigious** — stone manor with two **conical blue spire roofs**, gold
     finials and a small flag, arched stone doorway with steps, a cobblestone forecourt replacing
     part of the lawn, ornamental **blue crystal lamps** flanking the entrance, decorative
     planting. Instantly reads as the same family, three tiers up.
- **Row 2 — infrastructure kit pieces**: a straight cobblestone road tile with kerbs both sides;
  a **T-junction** tile with correctly curved kerb returns; a lamp post (stone pole, iron arms,
  glowing blue crystal, navy banner); a carved stone wall segment; a **blue crystal obelisk shrine**
  on a round stepped stone base.
- **Row 3 — vegetation & props**: a conifer with an understory bush and a boulder; a flower patch
  ringed by small stones; a **market stall** with a blue awning, barrels, crates and a lantern.

The lesson to score against: **a small number of reusable pieces, snapped to a shared plot
foundation, with unmistakable level-to-level silhouette and material escalation.**

## Reference D — District/biome tile sheet (the biome target)

Nine isometric district blocks, each on a square plot base with a stone kerb rim, showing that
**the same modular system re-skins per biome** while keeping streets, plazas and blocks:
1. Dense temperate town — many blue-roof cottages around a small central fountain.
2. Civic plaza — radial cobblestone paving, a **tall blue crystal monolith** centrepiece, domed
   gold-and-blue civic buildings.
3. Residential quads — four large plots, cobble streets between, blue-roof houses, conifers.
4. **Farmland** — windmill, golden wheat fields in rows, haystacks, barn, pond, dirt tracks.
5. **Coastal** — sandy shore, teal shallow water, wooden docks and moored boats, a lighthouse,
   net-drying racks, blue-roof cottages.
6. **Mining/alpine industrial** — gray rock, timber headframe, mine rails and carts, ore piles,
   a blue crystal seam in the rock face.
7. **Desert** — pale sandstone blocks with gold domes, palms, a turquoise oasis pool, awnings.
8. **Snow** — snow-capped roofs, deep snow ground, dark conifers with white load, a central
   campfire with warm light spill, log piles.
9. **Elven/verdant (swamp-adjacent)** — teal-green roofs, pale timber, a glowing teal fountain,
   lush overgrowth.

Every tile keeps the same legible ingredients: a **street/plaza network**, **kerb-bounded plots**,
one clear focal structure, restrained prop density, and a **coherent per-biome palette**.

## Reference E — Golden-hour close-up (material and lighting target)

Low warm sun. Player on a cobble path between four large plots. Shows:
- Heavy pale **stone kerb walls with capstones** defining every plot boundary — the strongest
  readability device in the whole reference set.
- Blue slate roofs catching a warm rim highlight along the ridges; deep blue-violet shadow sides.
- Gold interior light pouring from windows; a **market stall with blue awning** and a lit brazier;
  a **blacksmith anvil** and tools in a workshop yard; barrels, crates, a cartwheel.
- Flower beds, ivy, potted plants, tall grass tufts — dense detail *inside* plots, while streets
  stay clean and empty.
- Blue crystal finials on posts, plus a warm gold lantern — the **warm-gold vs blue-crystal**
  contrast is the signature of the art direction.

## Distilled acceptance criteria (use these when scoring)

**Camera / composition**: portrait 9:16; high 3/4 isometric (elevation ~50–60°); no perspective
distortion at the frame edges; player grounded in the lower third at ~3–5% frame height; horizon
and atmosphere occupy the top ~20%; the frame reads instantly as a navigable map.

**Palette**: deep navy-blue slate roofs; pale warm gray stone; cream plaster; dark timber;
mid-green grass; **warm gold** window/lantern light; **luminous cyan-blue** crystals and route;
restrained violet in shadows. No neon, no muddy browns, no gray-on-gray flatness.

**Readability**: every plot boundary is legible; streets are unobstructed and continuous; buildings
have distinct silhouettes against grass; nothing is visual mush; **fewer, larger** elements beat
dense clutter.

**Materials**: per-stone/per-tile shading is visible (not flat colour, not noise); surfaces read as
hand-painted, with soft value gradients rather than photographic detail or plastic specular.

**Lighting**: one warm directional key; soft cool shadows with visible direction; rim/edge light on
roof ridges and the player; atmospheric depth haze; **shadows never pure black**.

**Modular plot clarity**: the shared kerb-bounded plot foundation is obvious and repeated; buildings
sit squarely on their plot facing the street.

**Upgrade readability**: L0→L1→L2→L3 escalate simultaneously in height, silhouette complexity,
material richness (plaster → +timber → +stone/gold) and yard occupancy. Recognisable at thumbnail
size.

**Avatar visibility**: the player is unmistakable — dark navy silhouette with a light rim, on top
of a contrasting road, with a clean blue ground ring. Never lost in the terrain.

**Route**: a luminous blue path lying **inside** the street surface, following its real curvature,
not floating above it, not obscuring the cobblestone.
