Modular upgrade ladder — one shared plot foundation

Every level sits on the same kerb-bounded plot. Escalation must be simultaneous on three axes: height (1 → 2 → 2.5 storeys + spires), material richness (plaster → + timber framing → + stone base course & gold trim), and yard occupancy (bare grass → props → forecourt paving, planting, banner, crystal lamps). Silhouette alone must identify the level at 60 px tall.

Lighting & material rules

One warm directional key from the upper-left (azimuth ≈ 315°, elevation ≈ 40°, colour #ffe9c4). Fill is a cool hemisphere (#8fb3ff sky / #5a4a33 ground). Shadow sides settle to navy-violet #2b3a5e at roughly 22–30 % luminance — never pure black, never gray. Roof ridges and the player silhouette carry a pale-gold rim (#f2d9a8). Every surface must show per-unit shading — individual cobbles, individual roof tiles, individual plaster panels — with soft value gradients, not flat fill and not photographic noise. Depth haze (#8fa7bd) reduces distant contrast so the top of the frame recedes.
